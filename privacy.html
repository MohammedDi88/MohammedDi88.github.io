Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

“Lore of Things uploader” is a private tool used only by the owner of the Lore of Things accounts (contact: loreofthings1@gmail.com) to upload their own videos to their own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts, and to read the statistics of those videos.

What data is used. When the owner signs in with TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, the tool receives an access token and basic account information (account name, avatar, follower count, list of the owner's own videos and their view, like, comment and share counts). No data from other users is collected.

Where it is stored. Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local configuration file. Nothing is sent to third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

How long. Data is kept only as long as the owner uses the tool. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok, Google or Meta account settings, and the local file can be deleted.

Contact. For any question: loreofthings1@gmail.com
